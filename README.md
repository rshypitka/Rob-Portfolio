## Projects

### 1. [Loan Default Prediction (Capstone)](./Loan-Default-Prediction/)
**Classification | Risk & Compliance | Model Interpretability**

End-to-end credit risk modeling on the HMEQ dataset (5,960 records) to predict home
equity loan default under ECOA interpretability constraints. Prioritizes Recall to
minimize asymmetric default losses across Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient
Boosting, and XGBoost models.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `xgboost` `shap` `pandas` `seaborn`

---

### 2. [ExtraaLearn: Lead Conversion Intelligence](./ExtraaLearn-Lead-Intelligence/)
**Supervised Learning | Customer Conversion | GridSearchCV**

Predictive lead-scoring pipeline for an EdTech platform. Compares Decision Tree,
Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and XGBoost models tuned for Recall, paired with
converter profiling and outreach recommendations.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `xgboost` `pandas` `matplotlib` `seaborn`

---

### 3. [FoodHub: Order Data Analysis](./FoodHub-Data-Analysis/)
**Exploratory Data Analysis | Business Insights**

End-to-end EDA on a food delivery platform's order dataset. Analyzes order
patterns, delivery performance, customer behavior, and cuisine trends to surface
actionable insights for operations and marketing.

`Python` `pandas` `seaborn` `matplotlib`
