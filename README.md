## Projects

### [ExtraaLearn: Lead Intelligence System](./ExtraaLearn-Lead-Intelligence/)
**Binary classification | XGBoost | SHAP | Lead Scoring**

Predicts which leads are most likely to convert to paying customers for an EdTech
startup. Builds a probability-based tiering system (Hot / Warm / Nurture / Low)
that lets the sales team prioritize outreach. XGBoost final model achieved 0.88
recall and 0.927 ROC-AUC. Hot tier captures 73% of actual converters from 27%
of the pipeline.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `XGBoost` `SHAP` `pandas` `seaborn` `matplotlib`
